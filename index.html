This is all about the illusion of life.

There is no such power as designing a character. Everything is given character by itself, and no one deserves the title of designer, as if we could control the nature of things, or what others perceive when they look at what was made.

We call ourselves designers out of that frustration. We build characters, and a life behind them, as if it was not going to happen anyway.
